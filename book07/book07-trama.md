# Book 07 — The Museum of Unhappened Things / Il Museo delle Cose Non Accadute

## Title / Titolo
"The Museum of Unhappened Things" / "Il Museo delle Cose Non Accadute"

## Concept / Concetto
A surreal literary novel about Dr. Mira Solace, a 39-year-old curator who manages a museum dedicated to things that never happened. Each exhibit is an event that did not occur: a wedding that was never held, a war that was never fought, a conversation that was never spoken, a child who was never born. Mira catalogs these non-events with the solemnity of an archivist, but the exhibits begin to behave as if they did happen, and the museum begins to remember things that never existed. By the end, Mira realizes she is not curating the museum; the museum is curating her, and the unhappened things are more real than the things that did happen.

## Themes / Temi
The reality of absence, the museum as memory, the weight of what never happened, the curator as exhibit.

## Structure / Struttura
28 chapters in three acts: Collection (chapters 1-10), Contamination (chapters 11-20), Completion (chapters 21-28).

## Allusion Distribution / Distribuzione delle Allusioni
Every chapter contains 2-5 allusion markers referencing Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Proust, Hugo, and Perrin, with end-of-chapter explanation notes.

## Ending / Finale
Mira dissolves into an exhibit that never happened, and the museum continues. The reader realizes they have been reading a museum catalog that has become a novel. The ending is emotionally ambiguous: not sad, not happy, but impossible.
