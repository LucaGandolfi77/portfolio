# SHHH — Play Store Listing

## App Name
SHHH – Read in Silence

## Short Description (80 chars max)
The reader that needs silence. Read books & web. Noise punishes reading.

## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Transform your reading with ambient noise control.

**How it works:**
• SHHH monitors your environment via the microphone
• When noise exceeds your threshold, it blocks or fades the content
• Find silence. Read. Repeat.

**Reading Modes:**
• 🚫 Threshold — everything or nothing (hard block)
• 🫧 Fade — progressive transparency (soft blur)

**Supported Formats:**
• PDF — with PDF.js rendering
• ePub — with epub.js reader
• TXT, MD, HTML — with syntax-aware rendering

**Browser Built-in:**
• Browse Wikipedia, Project Gutenberg, Standard eBooks
• Noise-aware browsing

**Gamification:**
• Streaks with fire emoji 🔥
• Daily & total stats
• Focus timer
• Share your silence stats as an image

**Features:**
• 100% offline after first load
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• All audio processed on-device only
• Dark theme optimized for readability
• Italian & English localization

**Permissions:**
• Microphone — monitors ambient noise (never recorded or sent)
• Files — open shared documents

**Privacy First:**
• 100% offline — no server required
• No account, no tracking
• All processing on-device
• Your audio never leaves your device
• Free tier with non-personalized ads (removable with SHHH Pro)
