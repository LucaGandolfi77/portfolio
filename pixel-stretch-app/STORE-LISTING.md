## Short Description (80 chars max)

## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Transform your photos with creative pixel stretch effects.

**11 Unique Tools:**
• Stretch Radial — create radial pixel expansions
• Stretch Radial Full — full-radius radial stretch with alpha fade
• Stretch Row — stretch from any horizontal line
• Stretch Column — stretch from any vertical line
• Stretch Mirror — mirror columns or rows
• Twirl — rotate pixels with vortex effect
• Stretch Warp — free transform distortion
• Warp Grid 3×3 — mesh-based deformation
• Zoom — magnify details
• Move — pan the canvas
• Select — pick layers

**AI-Powered Features:**
• Background removal (on-device, no server)
• Two modes: Fast (basic) and Precise (AI model)

**Professional Editing:**
• Multi-layer composition
• 7 blend modes (Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Difference, Lighten, Darken)
• Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Blur filters
• Canvas resize with aspect ratio lock
• Undo/Redo history (50 steps)

**Export Options:**
• PNG (transparency support)
• JPEG (adjustable quality)
• WebP (modern format)
• Custom scale (0.5× to 3×)

**Privacy First:**
• 100% offline — no internet required
• No account, no sign-up, no tracking
• All processing on-device
• Your photos never leave your device

**Project Management:**
• Save/load projects (JSON format)
• Resume editing anytime

**Localization:**
• Italian (IT)
• English (EN)
