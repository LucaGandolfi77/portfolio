### Descrizione completa (4000 chars max)
Democracy is a game — and you're playing to win! Votopoli is a satirical political economy simulator where you climb from a tent to a castle through business, voting, and strategic corruption.

🗳️ DAILY ELECTIONS
Vote for mayors every day! Each candidate has unique tax rates, party bonuses, and favors. Your vote shapes your city's economy.

💼 13 IDLE BUSINESSES
From a lemon stand to a Media Company — buy businesses, earn passive income, and grow your empire. Each business generates money even while you're away.

🏠 8 HOME TIERS
Start in a tent, end in a castle. Upgrade your home to unlock more energy, longer offline earnings, and better perks.

🌍 20 COUNTRIES, 40 CITIES
Travel the world! Each country has unique bonuses: Switzerland boosts banking, France gets more strikes, Japan offers tech discounts.

🎮 7 SATIRICAL MINIGAMES
• Debate TV — answer political trivia
• Schiva la Burocrazia — dodge paperwork
• Campagna Elettorale — shake hands and kiss babies
• Busta Paga — catch money, avoid taxes
• Promesse da Marinaio — memorize political promises
• Debate Bingo — mark political phrases
• Tax Fraud Dodge — run from tax inspectors

🏛️ 5 POLITICAL PARTIES
Join a satirical party for passive bonuses:
• Partito del Pane 🍞 — food business boost
• Partito della Nanna 😴 — offline earnings boost
• Partito degli Automi 🤖 — tech business boost
• Partito del Caos 🎪 — minigame reward boost
• Partito della Romania 🏛️ — tax reduction

🏆 18 ACHIEVEMENTS
Track your progress with milestone achievements.

📊 DAILY CHALLENGES
Complete 3 daily challenges for bonus rewards. Build streaks for extra bonuses.

🔄 PRESTIGE SYSTEM
Reset for permanent income multipliers across 3 tiers: Democrazia, Repubblica, Impero.

🏗️ CITY SERVICES
As mayor, invest taxes in parks, schools, hospitals, courts, and airports.

👥 MULTIPLAYER
Connect with other players via P2P rooms in each city.

🌍 MULTILINGUAL
Available in 6 languages: Italian, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese.

📱 OFFLINE FIRST
Works completely offline. No account required. Your data stays on your device.

Votopoli — because in this game, the corruption is the feature, not the bug.
