## Short Description (80 chars max)
Interactive literary journey through 8 books by Emmanuel Carrère.

## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**VITE** is an interactive literary experience that takes you through 8 works of Emmanuel Carrère — one of the most important contemporary French writers — via 8 unique minigames.

**No scores. No pressure. Just reading and wonder.**

📖 **8 CHAPTERS, 8 BOOKS, 8 GAMES**

Each chapter explores a different book by Carrère, from his debut "The Moustache" (1986) to "V13" (2022). You'll read narrative passages, discover powerful quotes, and play a minigame that embodies the theme of each work:

• **The Train to Kotelnitch** — Reassemble a torn family photo
• **The Moustache** — Find what changed in each scene
• **I Am Alive, You Are Dead** — Reality or fiction? Swipe to decide
• **The Adversary** — Remove lies to reveal the truth beneath
• **The Kingdom** — Reconstruct fragments of Luke's Gospel
• **Lives That Are Not Mine** — Catch falling words of light
• **Yoga** — Breathe with a guided meditation circle
• **V13** — Listen and remember the words that matter

🧘 **CONTEMPLATIVE, NOT COMPETITIVE**

VITE is designed as a meditative experience. There are no fail states, no timers, no scores. The breathing exercise invites you to slow down. The "gentle" minigame has nothing to win — only moments to hold.

🏆 **15 ACHIEVEMENTS**

Track your journey through the literary universe of Carrère. Collect quotes in your Notebook, earn achievements, and reach the moving finale.

📓 **THE NOTEBOOK**

Every quote you collect is saved in your personal Notebook — a gallery of Carrère's most powerful words, organized by book.

🌑 **DARK MODE**

Read comfortably at night with full dark mode support.

🌍 **BILINGUAL (IT/EN)**

Switch between Italian and English with one tap. The entire experience — narrative, quotes, minigames — is available in both languages.

📡 **OFFLINE-FIRST**

VITE works completely offline. No internet connection required. Once loaded, everything is cached on your device.

👤 **ZERO DATA COLLECTION**

VITE collects zero data. No analytics, no trackers, no servers. Your reading progress stays on your device, in your browser's local storage. Your privacy is absolute.

—

**About Emmanuel Carrère**

Emmanuel Carrère (born 1957) is a French author, screenwriter, and filmmaker. Winner of the Prix Médicis 2025 for "Kolkhoze", the Princesse des Asturias for Literature 2021, and numerous other awards. His works have been translated into over 30 languages and adapted into films.

**Works included:** La vita come un romanzo russo, I baffi, Io sono vivo voi siete morti, L'Avversario, Il Regno, Vite che non sono la mia, Yoga, V13.
