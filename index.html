About Me

Hello! I'm Luca, a passionate full-stack developer with a love for creating innovative digital solutions. With years of experience in web development, embedded systems and AI. I specialize in building modern, scalable applications that make a difference.


My journey in tech started with curiosity and has evolved into a career driven by continuous learning and problem-solving. I believe in writing clean code, embracing new technologies, and creating user-centric experiences.
