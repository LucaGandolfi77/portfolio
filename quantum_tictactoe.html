Rules:
  • Quantum Move: Select 2 cells to place entangled marks.
  • Classic Move: Select 1 cell to place a permanent mark.
  • Collapse: When a cycle is formed (e.g. A-B, B-C, C-A), the board collapses into a single state randomly.
  • Win: Get 3 permanent marks in a row.
