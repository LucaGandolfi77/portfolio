# Brick-Wilds: Synth-Beast Chronicles

Isometric 3D toy-block diorama action-adventure PWA built with **Three.js + TypeScript + Vite 8 + vite-plugin-pwa**.

## Features
- **100% Procedural Brick-Built Diorama**: Terrain, stealth grass, water ripples, heroes, Synth-Beasts, and explosions are built from glossy ABS interlocking snap-blocks with 15 FPS stop-motion VFX.
- **4 Playable Block-Heroes**: Ayla (Pulse-Bow), Varn (Kinetic Spear), Tessa (Cluster Bombs), and Bram (Seismic Hammer), plus Holo-Visor weak-point scanning and 4 Snap-Gadgets.
- **Elemental Weak-Point Combat**: Shoot projectiles through campfires, shock crystals, or cryo-vents to infuse them mid-air and detonate detachable Synth-Beast components (including dropped Apex Rex Disc-Launchers).
- **Hearth-Haven Hub**: Customize 6 village plots with unlockable themes, swap costumes at the Wardrobe Booth, complete Community Board bounties for Core Cubes, and toggle Mod-Blocks.
- **2-Player Local Co-op**: Instant drop-in/drop-out co-op with Keyboard+Mouse, Split Keyboard, Touch Twin-Sticks, or Dual Gamepads.

## Commands
