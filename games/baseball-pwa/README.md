# DIAMOND & OVAL POLY '99 — 3D Baseball & Cricket PWA

Low-poly 3D stadium arcade PWA featuring **both Baseball and Cricket**, optimized for vertical iPhone portrait play and offline installation.

## Features
- **Baseball (`DIAMOND POLY '99`)**: Pennant Match (3 Innings), Home Run Derby (10 Outs), 9th Inning Clutch (Bases Loaded), 5 pitch types, Contact/Power/Bunt stances, baserunning sprint, advance, and dive slide.
- **Cricket (`OVAL POLY '99`)**: T20 Super Over (2 Overs, 3 Wickets), Sixes Derby (10 Balls), Last Over Chase (18 off 6), 6 realistic bouncing deliveries (Express Pace, Outswinger, Inswing Yorker, Leg Spin, Googly, Bouncer), Drive/Slog/Sweep shots, 3D shattering stumps & bails, 4/6 boundaries, running between wickets & crease dive.
- **Upgraded 3D Graphics**: Atmospheric Day/Dusk/Night skies with sun/moon bloom, drifting low-poly clouds, twinkling stars, volumetric stadium floodlight cones, live 3D Jumbotron scoreboard, cheering crowd, contact flash, screen shake, and tactical live field radar.
- **iPhone Portrait Optimization**: Safe-area insets, portrait-aware 3D camera framing, thumb-zone controls, relative drag aiming so your finger never blocks the reticle, and haptic feedback.
