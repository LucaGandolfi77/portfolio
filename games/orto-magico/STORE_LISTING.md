### Full Description (4000 chars)
Orto Magico is a relaxing idle garden simulator where you grow a thriving magical garden. Plant seeds, water them, and watch them grow — even when you're away. Discover 30 unique plants, brew enchanted potions, and build your dream green haven.

**IDLE GARDEN MECHANICS**
- Tap to plant seeds and water them
- Plants grow automatically over time
- Earn coins even when offline
- No pressure — play at your own pace

**30 MAGICAL PLANTS**
🌱 Starter Herbs — Basil, Mint, Rosemary, Thyme, Parsley
🌸 Flower Garden — Rose, Sunflower, Lavender, Tulip, Orchid
🍄 Rare Fungi — Shiitake, Morel, Truffle, Chanterelle, Porcini
🌳 Ancient Trees — Oak, Birch, Willow, Cedar, Cherry Blossom
💎 Crystal Flora — Moonflower, Starbloom, Sunpetal, Frost Lily, Ember Rose

**POTION BREWING**
Combine plants to brew magical potions:
- Growth Elixir — Speed up plant growth
- Golden Harvest — Double coin yield
- Rainbow Seeds — Grow random rare plants
- Dream Water — Attract butterfly visitors
- Phoenix Ash — Revive withered plants

**BUILD YOUR GARDEN**
- Unlock new garden plots
- Decorate with paths, benches, and fountains
- Attract wildlife (butterflies, birds, hedgehogs)
- Seasonal events with limited-time plants
- Garden rating system

**SEASONAL CONTENT**
- 🌸 Spring: Cherry Blossom Festival
- ☀️ Summer: Sunflower Fields
- 🍂 Autumn: Harvest Moon
- ❄️ Winter: Frost Garden

**RELAXING FEATURES**
- Ambient nature sounds
- Soft pastel visuals
- No timers or energy systems
- Optional notifications for harvest time
- Share garden screenshots

**PERFECT FOR**
- Plant lovers
- Casual idle game fans
- People who love Animal Crossing
- Anyone who wants a relaxing escape
- Garden enthusiasts

Built with vanilla JavaScript — zero dependencies, runs everywhere.
