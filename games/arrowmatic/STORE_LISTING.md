- Intuitive one-finger controls
- Non-stop auto-archer combat

- 600+ arrow types to discover across 6 elements

**ENDLESS REPLAYABILITY**

- 100+ permanent upgrades in the Forge

**UPGRADE SYSTEM**
Collect gold from defeated enemies to unlock permanent upgrades:
- Arrow Power — Increase base damage
- Quiver Size — Carry more arrow types
- Chain Bonus — Evolve with fewer matches
- Critical Chance — Land devastating crits
- Movement Speed — Dodge enemy attacks

**PERFECT FOR**
