## One-Line Pitch

> Learn photography, marketing, and social media through storytelling, collectible cards, and interactive minigames — set in a vintage Italian darkroom.

## Key Features

- 12 chapters of dialogue-driven storytelling through a vintage darkroom setting
- 36 photography and marketing concept cards with academic citations (Adams, Barthes, Sontag, Kotler, Cialdini)
- 12 interactive minigames: exposure triangle, white balance, composition, golden hour, selfie, hashtags, crisis management, campaign building, conversion funnel, agency management
- 18-question final exam with personalized certificate
- Endless agency simulation mode with 5 client types
- Full bilingual support: Italian and English
- Free to play, no in-app purchases required
- Built with Capacitor — runs natively on Android and iOS

## Branding Guidelines

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Dark Sepia | `#1a1208` | Primary background, dark mode |
| Gold | `#d4a853` | Accent, headings, highlights |
| Dark Gray | `#1e1e1e` | Text backgrounds, cards |
| Warm White | `#f5f0e8` | Body text, light surfaces |

### Typography

- Use clean, readable sans-serif fonts for UI
- Serif or handwritten fonts may be used for decorative/story elements
- Ensure sufficient contrast against `#1a1208` and `#f5f0e8`

### Logo / Icon

- Use the Esposta app icon (1024x1024 PNG for store submissions)
- Maintain minimum clear space around the icon
- Do not stretch, rotate, or add effects to the icon
