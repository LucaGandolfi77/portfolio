Controls

🖱️ Desktop: Move mouse to control paddle

📱 Mobile: Touch & drag to control paddle

⌨️ Keyboard: ← → ↑ ↓ arrow keys

⏸ Pause: Tap the button top-right or press Esc / P

How to Play

Score 5 goals to win! The puck speeds up over time. Combos multiply your score.

Power-Ups

🔵 Giant Puck becomes 2x larger for 5s

🟡 Slow-Mo Everything slows down for 4s

🔴 Multi-Puck 2 extra pucks appear (only original scores)

🟢 Shield Blocks your goal until hit once

🟣 Speed Puck goes 1.5x faster for 5s

🟠 Magnet Puck curves toward opponent goal for 6s

Combos

Score consecutive goals without the AI scoring. Each combo increases your score multiplier: x1.5 at 3 combo, x2 at 5 combo!
