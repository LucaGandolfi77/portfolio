Help

How to play

  1. Pick a match, market and stake, then simulate the quick match.
  2. Winner picks can cash out at half-time or Q3. Cash-out pays coins but no chest.
  3. A full win pays coins and awards a chest. Open chests to collect cards.
  4. Build a 5-card lineup. Its power and chemistry boost Winner and Margin picks.
  5. Cards upgrade with copies and coins. Wildcards add a copy of an owned card.

Progress

Every match, chest and mission grants XP. Your level never goes down and each level gives coins or wildcards. Trophies are your arena rank and can rise or fall.

Arena

Win trophies to unlock Pro and All-Star arenas, extra lineup slots and tournament rewards. Trophy road milestones grant coins and wildcards.

Markets

Winner picks a side. Margin picks a 10+ win or a close game. Total picks over or under the points line. Cash-out is available on Winner bets.

Special goals

Daily and weekly missions, the 2.00+ Daily Challenge, the 3-win Tournament and the Card Lab all grant extra coins and wildcards. The 7-day login track gives coins and wildcards, never chests.

Virtual only

Coins and chests are virtual. There is no real-money purchase, no server and no PvP.
