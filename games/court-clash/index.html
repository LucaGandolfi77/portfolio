Help

How to play

  1. Pick a team and a stake, then simulate the quick match.
  2. A correct pick pays coins and awards a chest.
  3. Open chests, collect cards and spend copies + coins to upgrade them.

Collection boost

Every 1,500 collection power gives +1 support boost to the team you back, up to +5. The boost improves your pick in the simulation.

Chest rules

Stake 50/100/250 gives Wood/Silver/Gold. Underdog wins and 3-win streaks add one tier, up to Legendary. Only one chest unlocks at a time.

Virtual only

Coins and chests are virtual. There is no real-money purchase, no server and no PvP.
