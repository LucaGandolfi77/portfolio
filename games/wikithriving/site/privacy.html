WikiThriving

Privacy Policy · Last updated: September 2026

Overview

WikiThriving is a Progressive Web App designed to teach life skills through gamified micro-lessons. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. All your learning progress, preferences, journal entries, and app usage data are stored entirely on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device:

  • Profile information (age, gender, country)
  • Learning progress and completed lessons
  • Game scores and achievements
  • Journal entries and gratitude logs
  • Garden plants and habits
  • Economy data (hearts, pearls)

You can delete all your data at any time by clearing your browser data for this app, or by using the "Reset Progress" option in the Profile settings.

Analytics (Opt-in only)

If you choose to enable analytics, we collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app:

  • Events like "lesson_completed" or "game_played"
  • Device type and browser version
  • Session duration

This data is never linked to your identity and is stored locally on your device. It is not shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

WikiThriving does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use cookies for tracking purposes.

Children's Privacy

WikiThriving is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect any personal information from any users, including children. The app functions entirely offline on your device.

Permissions

WikiThriving may request the following optional permissions:

  • Notifications (optional): For streak reminders. Can be disabled at any time.
  • Vibration (optional): For haptic feedback on achievements. Can be disabled in settings.
  • Audio (optional): For sound effects. Can be disabled in settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: privacy@wikithriving.com
