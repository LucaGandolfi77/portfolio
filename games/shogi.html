Shogi Rules

Objective

Capture the opponent's King (王).

The Pieces

  • King (王/玉): Moves 1 step in any direction.
  • Rook (飛): Moves any number of squares orthogonally. Promotes to Dragon (龍) (+1 step diagonally).
  • Bishop (角): Moves any number of squares diagonally. Promotes to Horse (馬) (+1 step orthogonally).
  • Gold General (金): Moves 1 step orthogonally or diagonally forward. Cannot promote.
  • Silver General (銀): Moves 1 step diagonally or straight forward. Promotes to Gold.
  • Knight (桂): Jumps in an "L" shape forward only. Promotes to Gold.
  • Lance (香): Moves any number of squares forward only. Promotes to Gold.
  • Pawn (歩): Moves 1 step forward. Captures forward. Promotes to Gold.

Drops (Paracadutaggio)

Captured pieces become yours! You can drop them back onto the board on any empty square as your turn, with a few exceptions:

  • Nifu (Two Pawns): You cannot drop a Pawn on a file (column) that already contains one of your unpromoted Pawns.
  • Drop Pawn Mate: You cannot checkmate the King with a Pawn drop.
  • No Mobility: You cannot drop a piece where it would have no legal moves (e.g., Pawn/Lance on the last rank).

Promotion

The promotion zone is the last 3 ranks (the opponent's camp). If a piece moves into, out of, or within this zone, it can promote (flip over) to gain new powers. Promotion is optional unless the piece would otherwise have no moves.
