### Full Description (4000 chars)
De Rerum Gatta is an educational puzzle/narrative game where you tend a secret garden where each flower represents a concept from mathematics or physics. To bloom a flower, read a story from a cat character, play a minigame that teaches the concept, and unlock an Erbario card with formulas, quotes, and practical applications.

**18 MINIGAMES WITH REAL SCIENCE**
Each minigame is based on actual mathematical and physical principles:
- 🌸 Fibonacci Petals — Discover the golden spiral in nature
- 🎵 Pythagorean Lyre — Explore musical harmonics and ratios
- 🍎 Newton's Apples — Launch projectiles with real gravity
- 🪐 Kepler's Orbits — Build elliptical planetary systems
- 🐱 Schrödinger's Box — Quantum probability made playful
- 🔢 Eratosthenes' Sieve — Find prime numbers step by step
- ⏰ Einstein's Time Dilation — Experience special relativity
- 📐 Archimedes' Lever — Move the world with fulcrums
- 🔥 Mendeleev's Elements — Sort the periodic table
- 🌊 Bernoulli's Fluids — Watch air pressure lift objects
- ⚡ Ohm's Resistance — Build simple circuits
- 🧬 Fibonacci's Rabbits — Understand population growth
- 🎭 Euler's Paths — Draw without lifting your pen
- 🌈 Newton's Prism — Split white light into colors
- 📏 Thales' Shadow — Measure using similar triangles
- 🌍 Galileo's Pendulum — Tune oscillation frequencies
- 💫 Kepler's Planetary Motion — Calculate orbital periods
- ⚛️ Einstein's E=mc² — Convert mass to energy

**A STORY OF LOVE AND SCIENCE**
6 chapters weave a love story between physics and a poetess, told through 6 unlockable letters:
- Prologue: The Secret Garden
- Chapter I: First Bloom
- Chapter II: The Lyre of Numbers
- Chapter III: The Dance of Planets
- Chapter IV: Light and Shadow
- Epilogue: Letters to the Universe

**ERBARIO (HERBARIUM)**
Each flower unlocks an Erbario card containing:
- The mathematical/physical formula
- A literary quote (Leopardi, Dante, Ovidi)
- A practical real-world application
- A philosophical reflection
- A deep formal explanation

**WALK MODE**
A meditative free-roam mode through your garden. No goals, no pressure — just the beauty of flowers and the company of cats.

**12 CONSTELLATIONS**
Light up the night sky by completing minigames. Each constellation tells a story from mythology and science.

**12 CALENDAR FLOWERS**
Monthly flowers bloom according to the real calendar — like Animal Crossing, but with math!

**FULL AUDIO EXPERIENCE**
Procedural Web Audio API synthesis — purring, chimes, meows, and clicks. Zero audio files. Works offline.

**PWA + OFFLINE**
Full Progressive Web App with service worker. Play anywhere, anytime, no internet required.

**PERFECT FOR**
- Students 14-25 (liceo + university)
- Curious adults who "hated math in school"
- Educators looking for engaging teaching tools
- Parents wanting educational screen time
- Anyone who loves cats and knowledge

Built with vanilla JavaScript — zero dependencies, 249KB total, runs everywhere.
