## One-Line Pitch
A turn-based JRPG with real-time parry combat, procedural 3D world, and a story about love, memory, and the cost of forgetting.

## Short Description (150 chars)
Three souls descend a tower of bells to save a dead woman. 5 zones, 5 bosses, 3 endings. Procedural 3D, zero asset files.

## Long Description
Echoes of the Last Dawn is a turn-based JRPG with real-time parry mechanics, built entirely in procedural 3D — zero asset files, 2,196 lines of custom code, runs anywhere.

In the city of Nivea, a bell tolls once a year for every person who will die. Tonight it rang twice — and the second toll bears the name of Mara Venn, protagonist Elia's sister, who died seven years ago.

Elia, Toma (who hates those who cling to the dead), and Iria (who has forgotten why she sings) descend into the Tower of Tolls to bring Mara home. What they find at the summit will force them to choose: erase all pain forever, or carry the memory of love even though it hurts.

## Key Features
- **Turn-based combat** with 3-person party
- **Real-time parry QTE** — time your press to the golden ring
- **5 unique zones** with procedural 3D worlds
- **5 bosses** with phase-2 transformations
- **8 NPCs** granting bonus skills
- **3 endings** based on player choice
- **Procedural audio** — no audio files
- **Save system** with 3 slots + auto-save
- **Full PWA** — installable, offline-capable
- **Touch-optimized** for mobile play

## Controls
- **Desktop:** Arrow keys to move, Space for parry
- **Mobile:** Virtual joystick + parry button
- **Universal:** Click/tap buttons for combat actions

## Tech Stack
- JavaScript ES Modules
- Three.js r160 (vendored, no build step)
- Web Audio API (procedural)
- Capacitor 6.x (Android + iOS)
- Service Worker (offline)
