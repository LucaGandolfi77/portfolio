Body:
Hey everyone! I'm Luca, an indie dev from Italy.

I just released Echoes of the Last Dawn (L'Ultimo Rintocco), a turn-based JRPG with real-time parry mechanics.

**What makes it unique:**
- Everything is procedural — no textures, no models, no audio files
- Turn-based combat with a real-time parry QTE (time your press to the golden ring)
- 3 characters, 5 zones, 5 bosses, 8 NPCs, 3 endings
- Built entirely in Three.js, runs in any browser
- Installable as PWA, works offline

**The story:**
In the city of Nivea, a bell tolls for everyone who will die. Tonight it rang twice — and the second toll bears the name of Elia's sister, who died 7 years ago. Elia descends into the Tower of Tolls to bring her home.

### r/gamedev
Title: Procedural JRPG postmortem: 2,196 lines, zero assets, full game
Body:
Just finished my procedural JRPG project. Some stats:
- 2,196 lines of custom JavaScript
- 3 files for the 3D scene (Three.js procedural geometry)
- 1 file for combat system
- 1 file for story/dialogue
- 0 external assets (everything generated from code)

Lessons learned:
- Procedural generation saves massive amounts of time
- Turn-based combat is easier to balance than real-time
- Parry timing is the core mechanic — it makes every fight tense
- PWA + Service Worker = instant install, offline play

Ask me anything about the technical implementation!
