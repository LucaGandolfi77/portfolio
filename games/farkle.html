Farkle
Roll the dice. Risk it all.

Goal

Be the first to reach the winning score (default 10,000) by rolling dice and banking points.

Each Turn

1.Roll all 6 dice.
2.Select scoring dice by tapping them. Selected dice glow blue.
3.Roll again with the remaining dice, or Bank your turn score.
4.If you score ALL 6 dice (Hot Dice), you keep all points and roll all 6 again!
5.If you roll and get no scoring dice (Farkle), you lose your turn score!

Key Rules

You must score at least 500 in a single turn before you can bank. You can keep rolling as long as you score at least one die each roll. Strategy: know when to push your luck and when to bank!

General

What is Farkle?
Farkle is a dice game where you roll 6 dice, select scoring combinations, and decide whether to bank your points or keep rolling for more. First to the winning score (default 10,000) wins!
What does "Farkle" mean?
A Farkle happens when you roll and get no scoring dice at all. You lose your entire turn score for that round!

Scoring

How do I score points?
Tap dice to select them. Only certain combinations score: each 1 = 100pts, each 5 = 50pts, three of a kind = face × 100 (three 1s = 1,000), three pairs = 1,500, straight = 3,000.
What is Hot Dice?
If you select ALL 6 dice as scoring, you keep the points and get to roll all 6 dice again for free!
Why is the Bank button greyed out?
You need to score at least 500 points in a single turn before you can bank. Select scoring dice until your turn score reaches 500+.

Tips

When should I bank?
It depends on risk! Banking early secures points. Rolling more dice has a higher Farkle risk. Generally, bank when you have 500+ and few dice left to roll.
What's the best strategy?
Always select 1s and 5s first. If you have 3+ dice left, it's usually safe to roll again. With 1-2 dice left, the Farkle risk is high — consider banking.
