🎨 NEURAL STYLE TRANSFER

How it works

Your photo's color statistics (means & deviations in a perceptual YCbCr space) are transferred onto the content using a Reinhard-style color matching, then each style applies its signature artistic filter — swirling strokes, geometric planes, mosaic pixels, gestural dabs or woodblock waves. The blend ramps from 0 → 100% live in your browser; nothing leaves your device.

Styles included

1. Starry Night — swirling skies, luminous oranges and yellows

2. Cubismo — geometric fragments, overlapping planes

3. Pixel Art — mosaic blocks, crisp edges, retro gaming aesthetic

4. Abstract Expressionist — gestural brushstrokes, emotional color fields

5. Ukiyo-e — japanese woodblock waves, elegant linework

Technical details

Perceptual color transfer (YCbCr, Reinhard et al.) + per-style procedural filters. 50 frames desktop / 20 mobile, blend eased 0→100%. Output PNG. A lightweight, dependency-free homage to A Neural Algorithm of Artistic Style (Gatys et al.).
