How it works

  • Upload: drop any audio file, then press Transcribe.
  • Record: capture from your microphone with a live waveform.
  • Live: instant dictation powered by your browser's speech engine.
  • The first Whisper run downloads the AI model (~41 MB), then it is cached for offline use.
