How it works

Every possession is simulated locally with deterministic randomness, tactics, fatigue and injuries. Use the tabs to play games, manage a team and explore analytics. Nothing is uploaded.

Quick start

  1. Pick a home and away team on the Play tab, then press TIP OFF.
  2. Watch the play-by-play, change speed, or press Skip to final.
  3. Open Season, Team, Explore or Cards only when you need them. The card economy is optional.

Modes

Season

An optional card game with virtual credits, chests, decks and a card league.

Numbers & jargon

OVR
Overall rating: a player's current ability.
OFF / DEF
Offense and defense ratings used by the simulation.
POT
Potential: how much a player can grow.
TS%
True shooting: scoring efficiency including free throws and three-pointers.
eFG%
Effective field-goal percentage: gives extra weight to three-point makes.
EFF
Efficiency: a single summary of positive and negative stats.
Credits
Virtual currency for the optional card mode. It has no real-world value.
Fragments
Currency used to craft a specific player card.
Pity
Bad-luck protection: a legendary card is guaranteed after enough pulls.
Wildcards
Items that add copies to a card so it can be upgraded.
Chemistry
A bonus for a well-balanced squad.

Keyboard

Space pauses or resumes, R starts a new matchup, M toggles sound, Esc closes dialogs.
